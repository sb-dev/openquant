# Robert Pardo, The Evaluation and Optimization of Trading Strategies — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `d51ba621d805`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This note draws on an assistant-written summary of the 2nd edition. It gives both pilot skills evaluation discipline: complete rule specification, declared and registered parameter searches, plateau and concentration checks, risk-adjusted comparison and walk-forward design. The proposer uses it to declare searches and invalidation conditions; the critic uses it to detect search overfitting, undeclared metrics and misused thresholds. Walk-forward re-optimisation needs a registered policy declaration before it applies, and multiple timeframes, margin, strategy-set capital and live monitoring are out of scope on the initial platform.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The summary rejects raw net profit as an optimisation objective (p1) yet gates on an annualised raw-return ratio (p2). Skills report walk-forward efficiency as a diagnostic beside risk-adjusted measures and never set or apply a gate the registered policy does not define.
- The 50 to 60 percent efficiency figure, any minimum trade count and any parameter limit are summary-only. Unless the registered policy adopts the rule, a finding resting only on them has primary category `method_advice` (OQ-02 §3.6, OQ-03 §4); a skill cannot cite this note to set thresholds.
- The Kaufman summary repeats the 50 to 60 percent efficiency figure as a robustness requirement (KAUFMAN-14), while this summary files it under a deployment-gate heading. Agreement between two assistant-written summaries verifies neither book; the registered policy owns any threshold (OQ-02 §3.1).
- Sizing starting capital from expected drawdown conflicts with policy-supplied capital and assigned strategy budgets (OQ-02 §§2.1, 2.4). Historical drawdown is an estimate, not a loss bound; OQ-02 §6.4 likewise treats loss limits as intervention thresholds, not guarantees.
- Testing across multiple timeframes conflicts with daily-only bars. Testing across instruments maps to separately registered watchlist runs: changing run membership creates a new trial (OQ-02 §2.1), and summed standalone results are not joint evidence (OQ-02 §2.4).
- The margin requirement in the summary's friction and capitalization row (p1) does not apply to cash-only long positions. Futures and leveraged instruments are out of scope.
- The summary assumes the researcher runs an optimiser. In OpenQuant the proposer requests bounded experiments and the engine runs them; each variant is a registered trial (OQ-02 §3.1, OQ-03 §2). Walk-forward re-optimisation needs a policy declaration before use.
- The seed fixes its parameters (OQ-02 §2.6) and has no in-sample optimisation step. Its threshold 30 and 32 versions are a declared comparison (OQ-02 §3.7), not a tuned grid; choosing the better one is still a disclosed search choice.
- A stitched out-of-sample curve is historical evidence. It cannot replace the final holdout or prospective paper evidence, and model training data may already contain the period (OQ-02 §3.3).
- This summary treats the stitched walk-forward curve as forward-looking; the López de Prado summary calls one chronological path insufficient. Walk-forward evaluation is required (OQ-02 §3.2); the policy may also declare CPCV or PBO (LDP-12). Neither summary decides, and a critic does not report the policy's chosen scheme as a defect.
- Plateau checks do not replace a multiple-testing adjustment such as DSR or PBO when the policy requires one (OQ-02 §3.1).
- Sharpe needs a risk-free basis, Sortino a target return and downside-deviation method, and annualised figures a periods-per-year convention. This summary states none, so skills must not assume one.
- Regime conditions used as entry filters need a reviewed causal operator. The initial operator set has none, so regime labels stay descriptive.

## Limits

- Excluded: the summary's empty simulator heading and its one-sentence introduction (p2), which describe an interactive widget with no content.
- Excluded as framing: the wording of the summary's actionable-rule column, its deployment-gate label, the claim that a skill should enforce a fixed pipeline, and the directive to check failure modes automatically.
- Rules whose operative instruction is a core-principle concept or one of the four failure modes the summary attributes to the book are `summary_claim_unverified`; instructions from framing, the actionable-rule column, pipeline steps or OpenQuant adaptations are `summariser_recommendation`.
- No rendering repairs were needed: the PDF has no raw LaTeX or damaged spans. The plateau table row is split across p1 and p2.
- Unverified numbers: the 50 to 60 percent efficiency figure and the outlier-trade count in the windfall failure mode. No book page has been checked.
- Primary check needed: the Return on Account denominator, how degrees of freedom are counted, the efficiency formula (pooling, annualisation, non-positive in-sample returns), walk-forward window design, the four market types, the model-efficiency measure and the divergence wording.
- Treated as the summariser's overstatement: the claim that a baseline confirms a structural edge, and the stitched curve described as forward-looking. The hindsight-bias claim sits in a failure mode attributed to the book; it is unverified and possibly overstated.
- Summary-only rules can support drafts and evaluation cases; they cannot enter a released bundle until checked against primary pages (OQ-03 §3).

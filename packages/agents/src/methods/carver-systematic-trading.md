# Robert Carver, Systematic Trading — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `5c95ed5c38d6`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This summary gives risk-first design checks: state a mechanism before searching data, treat estimated constants as fitted parameters, combine signals only on comparable scales, express costs in Sharpe units and avoid overstated loss or diversification claims. Both pilot skills can apply these to the daily long-only seed. Forecast-proportional volatility-targeted sizing, instrument weights with IDM, EWMA-based rules and no-trade buffers need reviewed operators (`needs_operator`); signed forecasts and futures or FX carry are `out_of_scope` on the initial platform.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The summary sizes from total Capital times an annual risk target. OpenQuant sizing divides the strategy's unreserved cash by vacant slots with lot rounding, inside its assigned budget; a proposal must never reference account-wide capital.
- The summary prefers continuous forecasts to binary entries with stops. The seed is binary with fixed ATR stop and target; a critic treats that preference as `method_advice`, not a concrete seed defect.
- The summary's EWMAC and EWMA volatility are different operator identities from the simple rolling means and simple-mean 14-bar ATR available initially. An SMA approximation is a new rule with its own identifier, not Carver's EWMAC.
- Signed forecasts between -20 and +20 imply short positions. The initial platform is long-only, so a translation must state how non-positive forecasts map to no position and register that as a different rule.
- The summary calls its constants fixed, yet FDM, IDM and weights come from correlations or weighting methods, and scalars target an expected absolute forecast. In OpenQuant each such value is fitted on training data only and registered as a search trial.
- The summary annualises with 256 trading days, 16 being its square root (CARVER-10), while the Kaufman summary divides by sqrt(252) (KAUFMAN-16). The registered evaluation policy owns the day count, and one comparison never mixes the two.
- The Aronson summary presents data mining as the route to objective signals; this summary requires a mechanism before any data search (CARVER-01). A mined rule is a registered trial needing search adjustment (ARONSON-04, ARONSON-05); a missing mechanism is `hypothesis_assumption`.
- Skew: this summary pairs trend rules with positive skew (CARVER-13); the Chan summary calls momentum positively skewed yet crash-prone (CHAN-10); the Gray/Vogel summary warns of momentum crashes (GRAYVOGEL-14). Time-series trend and cross-sectional momentum differ; skew is measured on registered data, never assumed.
- The one-third cost limit, the 0.13 figure, the 0.5-contract minimum and the half-Kelly cap are not policy thresholds. Under OQ-02 §3.6 a finding resting only on them is `method_advice` unless the registered policy adopts the rule.
- The summary does not state fill timing. Any translated rule enters at the next session's open under the registered policy, never at the signal close, and uses causal split-adjusted signal prices.
- Carry needs futures curves or interest-rate differentials between two currencies. An equity dividend-yield substitute would need reviewed dividend data semantics; dividends are accounted separately and never enter signal prices.

## Limits

- Excluded as summariser material: the opening claim that the method maps cleanly onto AI-skill instructions, the instruction that a skill enforce seven steps, the simulator sentence with no widget, and the closing reader question.
- The p3 AI-skill capability and gotcha lists are not book claims. They serve only as summariser advice (CARVER-06) or as consistency comparators (CARVER-09, CARVER-15).
- Unverified numbers: EWMAC scalars 10.6, 7.5, 5.3, 3.75, 2.65 and 1.87; slow lookback four times the fast one; carry scalar 30; expected absolute forecast 10 and cap 20; FDM and IDM caps 2.5; the 0.20 example risk target.
- Further unverified numbers: the 25-to-36-day volatility window, the 10% buffer, the 0.5-contract minimum, the one-third cost limit and 0.13 figure, and the half-Kelly bound of half the Sharpe ratio.
- The summary does not say how negative correlations are treated in the FDM and IDM matrices. Treat this as unresolved; primary check needed.
- Corrections still needing a primary-page check: the 256-day wording, `sigma_%` units, the turnover unit behind the factor of 2, the buffer definition, and volatility normalisation of carry versus other signals.
- Claims not adopted: fixed rather than estimated FDM, IDM and weights, general out-of-sample failure of mean-variance optimisation, orthogonal trend and carry, forecast reduction as a stop-loss, and 0.13 as a universal cost flag.
- The p3 line linking minimum contract sizes to half-Kelly limits gives no test or formula; it stays excluded as summariser AI-skill material.
- The summary gives no chapter or page locators for the book, and the edition is unknown; summary pages are the only citations.

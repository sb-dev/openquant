# Ernest P. Chan, Algorithmic Trading: Winning Strategies and Their Rationale — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `43465023abb8`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This summary supplies hypothesis discipline, backtest-hygiene checks and a contrast between mean-reversion and momentum behaviour. `strategy-formulation` can use it to name a mechanism and avoid timing, survivorship and cost errors in daily long-only definitions; `strategy-critique` can use it to detect those defects in a registered version. Stationarity tests, half-life lookbacks, z-score bands and external regime filters need reviewed operators or data first. Shorting, pairs and cointegrated baskets, Kalman hedge ratios, intraday, futures and FX methods, and Kelly or CPPI leverage are out of scope on the initial platform.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The p4 claim that CPPI guarantees a maximum drawdown conflicts with OQ-02 §6.4: loss limits are intervention thresholds, and gaps between rebalances can breach any floor. Kelly and CPPI set leverage and sub-account allocation; the §5 allocator owns allocation, and §§2.2 and 2.5 allow only unlevered cash-per-slot sizing.
- The p4 checklist's one-bar signal lag conflicts with OQ-02 §2.5, which already decides on the completed bar and fills at the next open. Adding it delays every decision twice; the seed's current-bar RSI and its volume mean that includes bar t are canonical (OQ-02 §2.6).
- The p4 blueprint rejects strategies that merely combine indicators and names a short list of acceptable drivers. Read as a closed list, it could reject the canonical seed, which combines SMA50, RSI and a volume mean; both skills instead test whether the contract names a mechanism for each component.
- The Aronson summary presents data mining as the route to objective signals, while this summary (p1–p2) and the Carver summary (CARVER-01) require a mechanism first. A mined rule is a registered trial needing search adjustment (ARONSON-04, ARONSON-05); a missing mechanism is `hypothesis_assumption` however the rule was found (CHAN-01).
- The 1.0–2.0 z-entry band, the 0.05 p-value and half-Kelly are neither defaults nor acceptance thresholds. The registered policy owns acceptance thresholds (OQ-02 §§3.1 and 3.6); a z-entry level is a definition parameter.
- Win rates differ: mean reversion 60–70% and momentum 30–45% here (p3); mean reversion 60–75% and trend following 30–40% in the Kaufman summary (KAUFMAN-09). Neither is a default, benchmark or gate (OQ-02 §§3.1, 3.6); the critic compares the measured hit rate with the exits' cost-inclusive break-even (CHAN-10).
- Stop advice conflicts: tight trailing stops for momentum and wide catastrophe stops for mean reversion here (p4), wide 3–4 ATR trend stops in KAUFMAN-09, structural and trailing stops in MURPHY-14. None is a default: OQ-02 §2.6 exits stay canonical, trailing stops are unavailable, and alternatives are registered variants (CHAN-11).
- Skew and crash claims differ: trend rules positively skewed in the Carver summary (CARVER-13), momentum crash risk in the Gray/Vogel summary (GRAYVOGEL-14), both here (p3). Time-series trend and cross-sectional momentum differ; skew and persistence are measured on registered data, never assumed from any summary.
- Long-short designs in the summary (cross-sectional reversal weights, decile momentum, time-series momentum that shorts, pairs) exceed the long-only platform. A long-only translation keeps one leg, changes market exposure and replaces proportional weights with cash per vacant slot; it is a new family.
- Hedge ratios differ in basis: the Kaufman summary hedges pairs with a return beta, while this summary estimates them on price levels with Johansen, Kalman or CADF (p2). Pairs need shorting and are out of scope (CHAN-14), so neither basis is adopted.
- The summary leaves the moving-average type and standard-deviation estimator of its z-score bands unspecified. The only initial average is the simple rolling mean; an exponential or Wilder-smoothed average, or a rolling standard deviation, needs its own reviewed operator identity and never reuses the seed's simple-mean RSI or ATR identifiers.
- Back- or ratio-adjusted futures contracts and settlement-price alignment have no equity counterpart in the seed. Equity signals use causal split-adjusted prices with dividends accounted separately (OQ-02 §2.6); a dividend-adjusted or total-return series would be a different data basis needing its own reviewed semantics.
- GRAYVOGEL-01 and GRAYVOGEL-02 already translate the Gray/Vogel summary's total-return momentum to price-only causal split-adjusted closes. Momentum ranks from either note therefore use the same lagged-close arithmetic with declared lags and skip window; neither may be presented as the total-return measure.
- Exits at z = 0 or at the opposing band, and trailing stops for momentum, are not among the initial exit operators in OQ-02 §2.2, which provide fixed stop and target levels from the entry ATR snapshot and a one-time stop change with a declared effective session.
- Randomised or Monte Carlo significance tests apply only when the registered robustness suite includes them (OQ-02 §3.2). A skill may request such a test, but it cannot turn the result into a gate or let it replace required evidence.

## Limits

- No book page was read. `summary_claim_unverified` means only that the summary attributes the content to the book; its chapter labels and the attribution wording that presents claims as the author's advice or stipulation are unverified.
- Excluded summariser material: p1 AI-skill framing, pitfall-auditor role and fixed test sequence, p3 portfolio-design framing, the p4 workflow structure and closing reader question. Blueprint items 2–4 appear only under CHAN-01, CHAN-06, Conflicts and Limits. No empty widget headings appear.
- Blueprint item 4 pairs mean reversion with CPPI drawdown floors, which cut exposure after losses, while the p4 table warns that tight stops convert temporary deviations into realised losses. This internal tension is unresolved; neither pairing is adopted.
- Rendering: formulas on p1–p4 appear as raw LaTeX, the p4 Kelly bullet contains a damaged span, and the Hurst row splits across p1–p2. Repairs here restore notation only; they are not book text.
- Corrections still needing a primary-page check: ADF null wording, the OU theta-lambda relation and half-life form, Hurst variance definition, Johansen exact weights, Kalman lookback claim, momentum skew sign, trade-shuffling procedure, Kelly excess-return definition and the CPPI guarantee.
- The summary writes Var(tau) proportional to tau^(2H) without naming the variable; the standard form uses the variance of tau-bar changes in log price, and H = 0.5 then describes a random walk in log price.
- Unverified numbers, none adopted: z-entry 1.0–2.0 (p2), lookbacks as small multiples of the half-life (p2), 0.05 p-value (p1), one-day reversal lookback (p2), win rates 60–70% and 30–45% (p3), 30–60 minute leveraged-ETF window (p3) and half-Kelly 0.5 (p4).
- Market-structure facts the summary mentions, including uptick rules, borrow constraints, the TED spread, VPIN and the GLD/GDX and EWA/EWC examples, were not checked and may be dated.
- The 2/7 break-even hit rate (CHAN-10), the mean-invariance point (CHAN-09) and the ranking-invariance point (CHAN-03) are this note's arithmetic, not summary or book claims.

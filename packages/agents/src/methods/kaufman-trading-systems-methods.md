# Perry J. Kaufman, Trading Systems and Methods — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `927af81180d0`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This summary of the 6th edition supplies indicator definitions, causal-timing hazards, execution-realism warnings and robustness checks. The `strategy-formulation` proposer uses them to keep seed operators exact and to plan bounded revisions. The `strategy-critique` critic uses them to detect smoothing mismatches, centred filters, same-close fills, optimistic stop fills and fragile parameters. Adaptive averages, regime filters, breadth data, stop-entry orders, trailing or time stops and volatility-scaled sizing need new reviewed operators, data or policy declarations. Futures, open interest, pairs and shorting, intraday methods, learned models and account-level sizing or allocation are out of scope on the initial platform.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The summary's EMA-smoothed ATR (p12) is a different operator identity from the seed's 14-bar simple-mean ATR, and Wilder smoothing is a third. Keep the seed operator; any other smoothing needs a new reviewed operator and an explicit revision (OQ-02 §§2.2, 2.6).
- The Wilder-labelled RSI (p5) states no smoothing or zero-loss rule. The seed RSI is a 14-bar simple mean with defined zero cases; a skill cites that operator and does not call it Wilder's.
- Centred seasonal factors (p5) and centred detrending (p6) conflict with OQ-02 §3.3, which allows only information available at the simulated decision time. A skill rejects them in signals.
- The summary's back-adjustment advice concerns futures rolls only. It does not justify dividend-adjusted signal prices: the seed uses causal split-adjusted signals and accounts for dividends separately (OQ-02 §2.6).
- p12 sizes from an unqualified portfolio-level Equity_t with weights w_i. Read as account or total-portfolio equity, it conflicts with OQ-02 §2.4; a strategy-ledger reading still needs w_i inside the assigned budget and a reviewed operator (KAUFMAN-16, KAUFMAN-18).
- Annualisation conventions differ: the Carver summary uses 256 days, with 16 as its square root (CARVER-10), while p12 here divides by sqrt(252) (KAUFMAN-16). The registered policy owns the day count, and one comparison never mixes the two.
- p10 makes WFE above 50–60% a requirement, and the Pardo summary files the same range under a deployment-gate heading (PARDO-13). That figure, the lint 4 and lint 5 numbers and the routing cut-offs stay advisory; the registered policy owns acceptance thresholds (OQ-02 §§3.1, 3.6; KAUFMAN-14).
- Sample sizes conflict: about 1,250–2,500 trades (p2) against 30 trades per parameter with up to four free parameters (lint 4), so 30 to 120 trades. Stage 2 separately prefers one or two primary parameters. Report the counts; gate on none of them.
- p10 and p12 call annualised return over annualised volatility an information ratio, with no benchmark, so it resembles a Sharpe ratio without a risk-free rate; p12 uses a compound (geometric) return. Calmar and MAR are merged, though their drawdown windows conventionally differ. Define metrics under OQ-02 §3.6.
- Win rates differ: trend following 30–40% and mean reversion 60–75% here (p10); mean reversion 60–70% and momentum 30–45% in the Chan summary. Neither is a default, benchmark or gate (OQ-02 §§3.1, 3.6); the critic compares the measured hit rate with the cost-inclusive break-even implied by the exits (CHAN-10).
- Stop guidance conflicts: wide 3–4 ATR trend stops (p11) with trailing exits (p13) here, tight trailing momentum and wide mean-reversion stops in the Chan summary (CHAN-11), structural and trailing stops in the Murphy summary (MURPHY-14). None is a default; OQ-02 §2.6 exits stay canonical; trailing stops are unavailable.
- Lint 8 could warn about the seed's 5 ATR target if the seed is read as trend following. The seed is a pullback-under-trend hybrid; treat such a warning as `method_advice` unless registered evidence shows a defect.
- p11 presents drawdown gates and wide stops as protection. OQ-02 §6.4 treats loss limits as intervention thresholds, and OQ-02 §2.5 fills a gap through a stop at the open, so neither caps a loss.
- Persistence claims differ: p1 and p9 say stocks and equity indices are noisy and favour short-term mean reversion, while the Gray/Vogel summary describes 6–12 month cross-sectional continuation (GRAYVOGEL-06). Horizons and constructions differ; persistence and skew are measured on registered data, never assumed from either summary.
- Hedge ratios differ in basis: p6 hedges pairs with a return beta under a cointegration heading, while the Chan summary estimates them on price levels (CHAN-14). Pairs need shorting and are out of scope, so neither basis is adopted.
- Lint 7 (no same-close fills) and lint 1 (distances in ATR or percent) agree with the seed and the platform. Agreement does not make them the book's content.

## Limits

- Excluded summariser material: the p1 heading calling the breakdown uncompressed, the p12 AI-skill framing of five primitives, and the mandatory framing of the four-stage pipeline and eight lint rules. Rules resting only on lint rules carry `summariser_recommendation`.
- The p1 bibliographic details (dates, page count, the eBook ISBN beginning 978-93, appendix contents) are unverified and unused.
- Rendering repairs: raw LaTeX, range markers and norm bars standing for absolute values were read as plain ranges and absolute values. Each repaired equation still needs a primary check before adoption.
- Primary check needed for: EMA ATR and its constant, RSI smoothing, the X-11 label, the ruin formula and its payoff ratio, TWR sign convention (MaxLoss negative), the WFE requirement, IR and Calmar or MAR definitions, VIDYA scaling and Hurst bounds.
- Back-adjustment (p10, lint 2) names the unadjusted spot price as the percentage denominator; the standard choice is the unadjusted price of the traded contract. Futures remain out of scope; primary check needed.
- VIDYA (p8) keeps its weight within [0, alpha] only if the Chande oscillator is scaled to [-1, 1], which the summary omits. Gann's 1x1 angle of 45 degrees (p7) depends on chart scaling. Neither was used.
- Division-by-zero cases the summary leaves open: RSI with no losses, ER on a flat path, CCI with zero deviation, Chaikin weighting when high equals low, and profit factor with no losing trades.
- Unverified numbers: 33–66% retracements, 70–80% CCI coverage, 3–4 ATR stops, win rates of 30–40% and 60–75%, payoff ratios above 2 and below 1, drawdowns above 50% under full optimal f, a 12% volatility target and a 4–5 timeframe ratio.
- Not made into rules. Out of scope: futures, open interest, COT, carry, pairs, shorting, Parabolic SAR, intraday and market-profile methods, learned models, portfolio allocation. Needing new operators, data or policy: stop-entry breakouts, trailing and time stops, breadth, adaptive averages.
- The seed's thresholds, slot count and ATR multiples come from OQ-02 §2.6, not from this source. Citing these rules is not evidence of profitability, and no rule is `primary_checked`.

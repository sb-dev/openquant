# John J. Murphy, Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `231c5d682601`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This summary supplies trend, volume, oscillator and breakout hypotheses for `strategy-formulation`, and checks on pivot timing, threshold provenance and reward-to-risk arithmetic for `strategy-critique`. Only rules expressible with simple rolling means, simple-mean RSI and ATR, comparisons, ranking, slot sizing and fixed entry-ATR exits apply to the seed today. Swing pivots, trendlines, chart patterns, ADX, EMA, MACD, Bollinger Bands, stochastics and OBV need reviewed operators first. Short selling, futures open interest, intraday candles, intermarket analysis and account-level money management are out of scope.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The summary's 3:1 reward-to-risk minimum would reject the seed's nominal 2.5. Acceptance thresholds belong to the registered evaluation policy (OQ-02 §3.6). A critic reports the ratio and may raise `method_advice`, not a concrete-defect finding, unless that policy adopts a minimum.
- The summary names a 14-period RSI, ADX and stochastics without stating smoothing. The seed's RSI and ATR are 14-bar simple means. A Wilder- or EMA-smoothed variant is a different operator identity and an explicit strategy revision, never a substitute under the simple-mean identifiers (see `KAUFMAN-02`).
- EMA, MACD, Bollinger Bands (standard deviation), stochastics (rolling high and low), ADX, OBV (an unbounded cumulative sum), swing pivots and pattern detectors are not initial operators; `MURPHY-16` governs proposals that need them.
- Stop advice conflicts: structural and trailing stops here (`MURPHY-14`); tight trailing stops for momentum and wide catastrophe stops for mean reversion (Chan summary); wide 3–4 ATR trend stops (Kaufman summary, `KAUFMAN-09`). None is a default: OQ-02 §2.6 exits stay canonical, trailing stops are unavailable, alternatives are registered variants (`CHAN-11`).
- Volume readings conflict: `MURPHY-07` takes above-average volume on recovery bar t as the trend resuming, while the Harris summary (`HARRIS-03`) treats high volume as possibly marking an informed, permanent move. The strategy contract must choose one reading and state the condition that would invalidate it (OQ-02 §2.1).
- The summary confirms breakouts on closes and rejects intraday penetrations (p2), but places buy stops just above round numbers (p2), and its four-week rule fires when price exceeds a prior high (p8). Here decisions follow the close and fills come at the next open, possibly well beyond the level.
- The p9 money management rules address total account equity, apparently in a futures-margin setting. Strategy sizing here uses only the assigned budget, cash divided by vacant slots with lot rounding; account-wide caps belong to portfolio allocation and the mandate (OQ-02 §§2.4 and 5).
- Weekly and monthly charts and the 40-week average (p6, p9) need calendar aggregation, which the daily operator set lacks. A 200-session simple mean is a different operator from a mean of weekly closes; do not present one as the other.
- The four-week rule (p8) is a stop-and-reverse system on calendar weeks. A long-only adaptation drops the short side, states its session count, decides on completed closes and needs a reviewed rolling-high operator; it is a new family, not a seed revision.
- Pyramiding and trailing stops (p9) conflict with one holding per instrument and the one-time stop change; the summary's fan lines (p2) are reversal signals, not stops. A proposal must not emulate either through repeated entries or extra stop moves.
- Downtrend definitions, bearish patterns and descending triangles are short-side material; on the long-only platform they support at most an eligibility exclusion built from initial operators, never a short entry. A sell signal used as a long exit needs a reviewed exit operator first (see `MURPHY-05`).
- Daily candlestick shapes are composable from same-bar and lagged OHLC references, arithmetic, min/max and comparisons, with a declared tolerance for near-equal open and close. The summary's restriction to structural zones needs pivot operators; its intraday candles are out of scope.
- Signals use causal split-adjusted prices without dividend adjustment. A percent breakout margin or round-number level must state whether it applies to the adjusted signal series or raw traded prices; after a split, a round quoted price in history is no longer round in adjusted terms.
- Each alternative the summary offers, such as a 1% or 3% margin, an ADX cut-off of 20 or 25, or a 9-bar RSI (beyond the reviewed 14-bar operator), is a separate registered trial once runnable. Choosing one after viewing results is search; consumed holdouts stay consumed.

## Limits

- Excluded summariser material: the system-prompt and rule-engine framing (p1), module labels, statements of what the AI must do, and the empty interactive router heading on p10 with no diagram.
- The five-gate pipeline (p9–p10) is not adopted as a workflow. Its numbers appear only as conflicting or corroborating evidence in named rules; where a rule rests on the pipeline alone, its corrections column says so.
- p1 announces six Dow Theory tenets but lists four bullets. The missing tenets are not reconstructed here; primary check needed.
- Flags and pennants: p4 gives a pause of one to three weeks at most, while p4–p5 mark a pattern suspect only after four weeks. Unresolved; primary check needed.
- Key reversal day (p4): the summary requires the close to fall under the previous session's low; common usage requires only a close under the previous close, and the stricter form also makes the bar an outside day. Primary check needed.
- Rendering repairs only: raw LaTeX in the trend formulas (p2), the Bollinger plus-or-minus sign (p6), and the reward-to-risk ratio, relative-strength ratio and pyramid arrows (p9). Repaired readings remain unverified.
- p8 and p9 label 50% a Fibonacci retracement; it is not a Fibonacci ratio, though often used beside 38.2% and 61.8%. The 33% and 66% bands (p1–p2) are separate thirds.
- The doji definition (p8) demands exact equality of open and close; common usage allows a small difference, so any translation needs a declared tolerance.
- Unverified numbers: trend durations (p1), retracement bands (p1–p2), 3% and 1% filters (p2), double-top spacing and depth (p3), triangle breakout zone and wedge duration (p4–p5), hammer shadow ratio and Elliott ratios (p8), money management figures (p9).
- Chapter references (16 and 19) and the book attribution of the RSI bull and bear ranges, the regime hierarchy and the four-week rule are unverified; primary check needed.
- Not converted into rules: Elliott wave constraints, time cycles and translation, intermarket relationships, sector rotation, futures open interest and relative strength against a benchmark. Each needs data or operators outside the initial platform.
- Overstated claims listed under `MURPHY-01` remain hypotheses to test. No note here is evidence of profitability, and summary-only rules cannot enter a released bundle.

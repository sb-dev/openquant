# Stephen H. Penman, Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `d319deca82e9`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This 5th-edition summary contributes accounting-based valuation ideas: separating operating from financing items, decomposing profitability, residual operating income valuation and reading a market price as implied growth. In the pilot skills it mainly supports the critic when a hypothesis, rationale or source reference cites fundamentals, and it stops the proposer from giving a price-only rule a fundamental rationale. No initial operator reads financial statements, so fundamental quantities cannot enter a Strategy Definition until filing-timed data semantics and a reviewed operator exist. Short positions on overvaluation readings, the summary's buy/sell signal and account-level capital decisions are out of scope.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The summary treats statement data as simply available. OQ-02 §1.1 and §3.3 require company ID, accession, acceptance time, amendment relation and restatement resolution at the cutoff, and OQ-02 §2.2 has no fundamental operator. Keep Penman quantities in research evidence and critique until one is reviewed.
- For absent footnotes, p6 assumes operating cash at 1% of sales and the statutory tax rate. These replace filing facts; under OQ-03 §4 record each as an unresolved assumption or mark evidence insufficient. Its zero terminal unusual and OCI items are a forecast assumption to declare.
- The closing offer of a Python script for reformulation and valuation conflicts with OQ-02 §2.2 and OQ-03 §§2–3, which allow no generated or evaluated code. Any such calculation must become a reviewed, typed operator with tests.
- The enterprise-value step adds market capitalisation to book NFO. Market capitalisation needs the share count effective at the price date under causal split handling (OQ-02 §2.6), and the latest filed NFO is a stale book proxy for debt value; record both sources and dates.
- The summary's net payout d is a company-level clean-surplus flow. It does not alter strategy return accounting, where dividends are separate entitlements and signal prices are never dividend-adjusted (OQ-02 §2.6).
- Financial leverage in the summary describes a company's capital structure. It does not permit margin or borrowed exposure in a strategy, which stays long-only in cash and sizes from its assigned budget, never account-wide capital (OQ-02 §§2.4–2.5).
- The p4 column that routes valuation models to firm types and the p5 instruction about apparent bargains are summariser material. Method notes cannot set finding treatment or acceptance thresholds; the registered policy does (OQ-02 §§3.1, 3.6).

## Limits

- Excluded summariser material: the p1 framing of an AI skill and its guardrail wording, the p4 model-routing column, the p6 blueprint (triggers, reconciliation display, default conventions) and the closing reader question offering a Python script.
- Unverified numbers: working cash of 0.5%–2% of sales (p2), terminal value above 80% of value (p1), long-run growth of 3%–4% (p4–p5), baselines g = 1.0 and 1.04 (p6) and operating cash at 1% of sales (p6).
- The p4 claim that residual income and earnings-growth models match dividend-discount values needs clean surplus, consistent full-horizon forecasts, and equity (ρ_E) and operating (ρ_W) rates consistent with leverage, with NFO at fair value. Primary check needed.
- Overstatements not adopted: ReOI valuation shielding value from leverage changes (p4) assumes fairly priced debt and an unchanged operating discount rate; deriving free cash flow from accruals (p3) removes classification errors only if the reformulation is right.
- Rendering repairs: p1–p6 show raw LaTeX markup, and the p1–p4 tables wrap formulas across lines, with one p1 formula continuing on p2; formulas were read from that markup.
- Internal-consistency fix: the p3 inverse-turnover list keeps receivables, inventory and PP&E less payables, omitting working cash, intangibles, deferred tax assets and the other p2 operating liabilities, so it does not sum to 1/ATO = NOA/Sales. Primary check needed.
- The p4 table offers AEG for testing analysts' EPS forecasts, but its formula uses OI and ρ_W, an unlevered basis. Compare levered EPS only after removing the leverage effect (PENMAN-05). Primary check needed.
- Chapter numbers (5 through 19) are the summary's own attributions and are unverified; rows cite summary pages only.
- Corrections still needing a primary-page check: noncontrolling interest treatment, after-tax NFE netting, balance timing, growth notation, the levered P/E condition, the OLSPREAD definition, the inverse-turnover list and the AEG earnings basis.
- Classification claims (pension and OPEB liabilities and deferred taxes as operating; preferred stock as a financial obligation) are summary claims only.

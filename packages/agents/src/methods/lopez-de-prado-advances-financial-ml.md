# Marcos López de Prado, Advances in Financial Machine Learning — summary-derived method notes

Source: summary PDF `c5a10e4afb55`, recorded in `packages/agents/src/methods/sources.json`. Draft; no rule has been checked against the book.

## Scope

This source gives both pilot skills leakage and multiple-testing checks. It covers segment boundaries and embargoes, overlapping positions, volatility-scaled exits (snapshot timing from OQ-02 §2.6) and deflated Sharpe statistics fed from the registered search history. The proposer uses it to avoid backtest tuning and to declare the seed's missing time exit; the critic uses it to detect leakage and weak search adjustment. Activity and information-driven bars, microstructure and entropy features, learned models, meta-labelling, probability bet sizing and hierarchical risk parity across strategies are out of scope. Fractional differencing and volatility-scaled sizing within one strategy need new reviewed operators. Combinatorial splits, PBO and synthetic-path evaluation need policy declarations, which a proposal may request.

## Conflicts and adaptations

- The summary leaves barrier volatility unspecified; the seed uses the 14-bar simple-mean ATR snapshotted at the signal session. An exponentially weighted, Wilder or standard-deviation estimator is a different operator identity and cannot reuse the simple-mean ATR identifier (OQ-02 §2.2).
- The summary's triple barrier labels training data; the seed's stop and target are executable exits. Labelled outcomes do not establish fills: gap-through stops fill at the open with adverse costs, and the stop wins ambiguous bars (OQ-02 §2.5).
- In the summary only meta-labelling's primary model chooses long or short sides; the triple barrier is described in a long-side layout, profit-taking above and stop-loss below. M1 is long-only cash; a seed translation drops short-side selection and records it as a source-to-draft difference.
- The summary claims its pipeline removes backtest overfitting and frames the p3 guardrail rules as mandatory directives. OQ-02 §3.1 says no statistic alone proves an edge; OQ-02 §3.6 lets only the registered policy set blocking treatment, never a method note.
- The summariser's p3 rule column sizes the embargo as a percentage with no stated base. OpenQuant aligns by session, never by row number (OQ-02 §2.5), so a declared embargo counts common-calendar sessions, and warm-up stays separate from the scored period.
- The Pardo summary presents the stitched walk-forward curve as forward-looking evidence (PARDO-12); this summary calls one chronological path insufficient. Walk-forward evaluation is required evidence (OQ-02 §3.2); CPCV or PBO are optional additions the policy may declare (LDP-12). A critic does not report the policy's chosen scheme as a defect.
- Chronological walk-forward splits need boundary handling for open positions (LDP-01) but no look-ahead embargo. An embargo (LDP-02) applies only when a declared validation segment precedes later selection data.
- Imported results must disclose unknown external search (OQ-02 §3.1). A deflated Sharpe ratio computed on them has an incomplete trial count and may overstate significance; say so.
- The summary gives no acceptance thresholds. Any PSR, DSR or PBO cut-off belongs to the registered evaluation policy, never to this note or a proposal.
- A fractional differencing operator, if added, must run on the causal split-adjusted signal series, never dividend-adjusted prices (OQ-02 §2.6), under its own operator identity.
- The sequential bootstrap serves ensemble bagging. Robustness resampling is whatever the cohort policy declares (OQ-02 §3.2); a critic reports overlap-blind resampling against that policy, not as a requirement of this source.
- The summary uses CUSUM for a structural-break test on p2 and an event-sampling filter on p3. These are different methods; a citation must name which, and neither is an initial operator.

## Limits

- Excluded as summariser material: the p1 sentence on turning the book into an AI skill, the p3–p4 unified-versus-four-skill architecture and its skill names, and the closing reader question on p4.
- The whole p3 guardrail table, all three columns and its framing, is a summariser lint list with no chapter citations. Rules cite p3 only to corroborate claims attributed on p1–p2.
- The summary's directive wording addressed to a skill or agent (p1–p2) is summariser phrasing, not a book claim; these rules keep only the attributed method content.
- Chapter structure is unverified. One internal inconsistency: the summary counts 22 chapters but assigns only Chapters 2–22. Merging Parts 4 and 5 and labelling Chapters 20–22 as multiprocessing are unverified, not inconsistent. Primary check needed.
- Unverified numbers: the 2018 publication year, chapter count and ranges, chapter numbers attached to each method, and the 0-to-1 range for d.
- The summary bounds bagging max-samples by average uniqueness on p1 but sets it equal to average uniqueness on p3. Out of scope here and unresolved.
- Corrections still needing a primary-page check: PSR and DSR formulas and which adjusts for trials, PBO via combinatorially symmetric cross-validation, average uniqueness, SFI joint effects, barrier volatility estimator and timing, d = 1 (LDP-13), and the synthetic-path claim (LDP-09).
- No rendering repairs were needed: the PDF shows no raw LaTeX or damaged spans. Corrections are internal-consistency fixes or checks against standard definitions and the OpenQuant specifications.
- Covered without rules because out of scope: structural-break, entropy and microstructure features; hyperparameter search scored by log-loss or F1; PCA orthogonalisation; multiprocessing. None has a reviewed initial operator.
- The attributed warning that likens backtesting during research to drinking and driving (p2) is paraphrased; its wording and attribution are unverified.

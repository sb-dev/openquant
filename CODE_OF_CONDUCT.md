# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss evidence and ideas rather than personal traits. Welcome questions and constructive disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing private information are prohibited. Do not use community spaces for signal sales, stock tips, copy-trading promotion, or investment solicitation.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict repeat offenders. Decisions should explain the behaviour and action taken while protecting private information.

Report concerns through the private maintainer contact established during repository setup. If no private contact is available, request one without including personal or sensitive details in public. Record that contact before inviting a public community.

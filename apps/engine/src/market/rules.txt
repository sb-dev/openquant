alpaca-daily-normaliser version 1

These rules turn the responses of the Alpaca adapter into one dataset-manifest/1 snapshot. The
SHA-256 of this text is the transformation digest of the manifest (OQ-05 section 7.1). Any change
to the text is a new digest and a new normaliser version.

Inputs. The profile's data semantics, the instrument mapping, the requested calendar range, the
provider asset record, calendar, daily bars and corporate actions, and the response bytes.

1. Instrument. The asset id equals provider_asset_id, the asset symbol equals the mapping symbol,
   the asset class is us_equity, and instrument_id equals "alpaca:" followed by the asset id.
   Otherwise the profile is blocked.

2. Calendar. A session is a provider calendar row. Its open_at and close_at are the local New York
   open and close converted to UTC with the US Eastern rule (daylight time from 02:00 on the second
   Sunday of March to 02:00 on the first Sunday of November). A holiday, a weekend and an
   unscheduled closure are absent from the provider calendar and therefore absent from the
   snapshot; an early close is a session with its early close time. The snapshot calendar runs from
   the first requested date to the later of the last requested date and the payment session of the
   latest kept cash dividend.

3. Bars. A bar is stamped with local midnight New York of its session; any other stamp blocks the
   profile. The session must be in the calendar; otherwise the profile is blocked. Open, high, low
   and close are positive decimals not above 1000000000, low is not above open or close, high is not
   below open or close. Volume is a non-negative integer not above 1000000000000000. Numbers keep
   their exact value and are written as minimal decimal strings (no exponent, no trailing zeros).
   Two bars of one session are one bar when identical; otherwise the profile is blocked.

4. Bar times. event_time is the session close_at. available_at is close_at plus the declared
   data-ready delay in minutes. On an early-close day close_at is the early close. A bar is kept
   even when its available_at is after the information cutoff of a run; the core decides
   FUTURE_DATA.

5. Coverage. The expected sessions are the calendar sessions inside the requested range. The
   missing sessions are the expected sessions without a bar. COMPLETE: none missing and at least
   one bar. PARTIAL: some missing. NO_OBSERVATIONS: no bar. An absent bar never creates a halt:
   unplanned_events is always empty because no halt evidence source exists.

6. Corporate actions. Only actions whose ex-date is inside the requested range are kept, and none
   is dropped for any other reason. The ex-date must be a calendar session. Types: forward and
   reverse splits are SPLIT; cash dividends are CASH_DIVIDEND, or SPECIAL_DISTRIBUTION when the
   provider marks them special or foreign; any other type blocks the profile, because the profile
   declares an availability rule for splits and dividends only.

7. Action availability. For every kept split and cash dividend the provider field named by the
   profile rule must hold a calendar date strictly before the ex-date. Then available_at is the
   open_at of the ex-date session. A missing, unreadable or not-earlier date blocks the profile. No
   fixed offset, record date, process date or retrieval time ever replaces it.

8. Dividend payment. payment_session is the provider date named by the payment rule. It must be a
   session of the provider calendar and not before the ex-date, otherwise the profile is blocked.
   payment_phase is AFTER_CLOSE. The amount is the provider rate as an exact decimal, positive,
   with at most 8 fractional digits, otherwise the profile is blocked.

9. Splits. ratio_new and ratio_old are the provider new_rate and old_rate reduced to the lowest
   integer ratio. Both must be within 1 and 1000, otherwise the profile is blocked.

10. Action identity. Actions are ordered by ex-date, then splits before dividends, then provider
    id, then the remaining provider fields, so the order never depends on the provider's list
    order. action_id is split-<symbol>-<ex-date> or div-<symbol>-<ex-date> with the symbol in
    lower case, and -2, -3 and so on for further actions of one kind and day in that order.
    provider_record_id is the provider id when it is a valid identifier, otherwise "alpaca:" plus
    the action_id. action_evidence records the profile field name and the provider value.

11. Price scale. The smallest scale that holds every open, high, low and close exactly. Above 4
    blocks the profile. A scale of 3 or 4 needs the S1 overflow evidence before use.

12. Identity. dataset.id names symbol, timeframe, adjustment, feed and the requested range.
    dataset.version is "c" and the first 16 hex characters of the canonical digest of the content.
    A source response is listed with the SHA-256 of its exact bytes, by the sequence of its call.
